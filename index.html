💡 Why the "Old way (1997)" Failed

Early search engines (like AltaVista and Lycos) ranked pages purely by keyword repetition. Spammers quickly exploited this by repeating keywords dozens of times (often in invisible white text), easily gaming the algorithm and stealing rank #1 from quality websites.

🚀 Why the "Surfer way (1998)" Revolutionized Search

Larry Page and Sergey Brin modeled the web as a directed graph. A random surfer traverses links: authority pages receive lots of incoming links from other pages, accumulating visits. Spam pages with zero incoming links get demoted to the bottom regardless of keyword stuffing!
